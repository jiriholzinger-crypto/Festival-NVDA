Festival synthesizer for NVDA

This add-on lets NVDA speak with the Festival Speech Synthesis System.

It is a port of Olga Yakovleva's Festival synthDriver (2008–2011) to current NVDA versions.

Included voices

  • English: kal_diphone, ked_diphone
  • Czech: czech_ph, czech_dita, czech_krb, czech_machac

Requirements

  • 64-bit NVDA 2026.1 or later.
  • Windows 10 or 11.

How it works

Festival 2.5.1 (festlib.dll 2.1, 64-bit) runs in the small helper program festhost64.exe. It sends the synthesized speech back to NVDA, which plays it like the speech of its built-in synthesizers. If Festival crashes, only the helper stops: NVDA keeps running and the helper is started again with the next speech. After three crashes within a minute the add-on stays silent until NVDA is restarted or the synthesizer is selected again.

Settings

  • Voice, rate, pitch and volume are in NVDA's Speech settings and in the synth settings ring.
  • The first time Festival is selected, the voice matching NVDA's language is used: czech_ph for Czech, kal_diphone for English, czech_ph for other languages. After that NVDA remembers the chosen voice.
  • Rate 50 is the voice's own tempo; 0 is half speed and 100 double speed.
  • Pitch 50 is the voice's own pitch; 0 and 100 are one octave lower and higher.
  • "Capital pitch change percentage" works: capital letters are spoken with a higher pitch.
  • Speech goes to the audio output device selected in NVDA, and audio ducking applies to it.
  • Index reporting (say all, moving the caret during say all) and pausing speech (Shift) work.
  • festsettings.xml in the add-on folder sets the code page and text replacement rules for each voice. Czech voices use ISO-8859-2 (cp 28592). Replacement rules are ECMAScript regular expressions; rules that cannot be read (for example Perl look-behind) are skipped and noted in festival.log.

Downloading more voices

Open the NVDA menu, Tools, Festival voices.... The dialog lists the voices from festvox.org/packed/festival/2.5/voices with their size and whether they are included with the add-on or already installed.

  • Install downloads and unpacks the selected voice. Festival is restarted, then choose the new voice in NVDA's voice settings (NVDA+Control+V).
  • Some voices need an extra pronunciation dictionary. The British English voice rab_diphone needs the OALD dictionary; the dialog offers to download it. Its licence allows non-commercial use only.
  • Remove deletes a downloaded voice. Voices that come with the add-on cannot be removed.
  • Open voice folder opens the folder with the downloaded voices.

Downloaded voices and dictionaries are kept in the festivalVoices folder of the NVDA user configuration (for example %APPDATA%\nvda\festivalVoices), so they stay when the add-on is updated. You can also unpack other Festival voices there yourself, in the form voices\<language>\<voice>\festvox\<voice>.scm.

Each voice has its own licence, included in its folder. Most festvox.org voices are free to use; the OALD dictionary is for non-commercial use only.

Notes

  • Festival messages and errors are written to festival.log in the add-on's synthDrivers\festival folder.
  • If the helper crashes, the place of the crash is written to festhost-crash.log in the same folder, and NVDA's log says "festhost64.exe stopped unexpectedly". Please send both files when reporting a problem.

Changes

2026.11.5

  • New dialog in the NVDA Tools menu to download, install and remove voices from festvox.org, including the OALD dictionary for the British English voice.
  • Downloaded voices are kept outside the add-on, so they survive add-on updates.
  • Czech translation of the add-on.

2026.11.4

  • Default voice follows NVDA's language (czech_ph in Czech NVDA) instead of always English kal_diphone.

2026.11.3

  • Festival runs in the 64-bit helper process festhost64.exe again, so a crash in Festival cannot close NVDA. The helper restarts automatically and records crashes in festhost-crash.log.
  • Speech audio is still played by NVDA (output device, ducking, exact index reporting, pitch).

2026.11.2

  • Rate 50 is now each voice's natural tempo (the English voices kal and ked were 10% too fast).
  • Smoother rate scale: 0 = half speed, 100 = double speed (0 used to be ten times slower).

2026.11.1

  • New 64-bit festlib.dll 2.1 loaded directly into NVDA; festhost.exe is no longer needed.
  • New pitch setting and support for capital pitch change.
  • Speech is played by NVDA: output device, audio ducking and exact index reporting.
  • Rate and pitch are kept when the voice changes.
