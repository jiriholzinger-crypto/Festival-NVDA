This synthesizer driver allows NVDA to use Festival Speech Synthesis System. Copy the contents of the synthDriver directory into the user's synthdrivers directory.
Two  English voices have been included: kal_diphone and ked_diphone.
You can find six additional English voices at
http://hts.sp.nitech.ac.jp/?Download
Unpack the downloaded archives into the synthDrivers\festival directory. The archives contain *.tar files. First extract these files, then unpack them.
You can also download the following voices, but they are big, not very responsive and their initialization takes a very long time, although they sound much better:
http://festvox.org/packed/festival/latest/festvox_cstr_us_awb_arctic_multisyn-1.0.tar.gz
http://festvox.org/packed/festival/latest/festvox_cstr_us_jmk_arctic_multisyn-1.0.tar.gz
Unpack the archives into the synthDrivers directory.
